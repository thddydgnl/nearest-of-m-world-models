\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\textbf{\Nom{} and related objectives.} IMLE matches each data point to its nearest generated sample \citep{li2018imle}. Conditional IMLE \citep{li2019diverse,li2020multimodal} was designed for conditions with one realized outcome, on which conditional GANs ignore their noise input, and is argued not to collapse modes. With a small $m$ its generator memorizes the outcomes of identifiable contexts (Section~\ref{sec:results2}). Later variants reject already-close samples, anneal a per-sample radius, split the noise hierarchically, or add a robust loss \citep{vashist2024rsimle,aghabozorgi2023adaptive,peng2022chimle,vashist2026roms}. IMLE serves as a world model on state vectors \citep{aghabozorgi2026wimle}, a trajectory generator for model-predictive control \citep{lee2026imlempc} and an action head \citep{rana2025imlepolicy,hosseinkhani2026imlevla}. \citet{gladstone2026xm} study the min-of-$K$ objective as a pretraining axis, include a Maze2D world model with one to four network evaluations, and find that more candidates reduce overfitting on video, which they attribute to expressivity. Conditional min-of-$N$ objectives go back to \citet{fan2017pointset} and \citet{bhattacharyya2018bestofmany}, and multi-hypothesis feature forecasting to \citet{vondrick2016anticipating}. Winner-takes-all and multiple-choice learning \citep{guzman2012mcl,lee2016smcl,rupprecht2017mhp,makansi2019ewta,seo2020tmcl} learn a fixed number of heads. Their optima are conditional quantizers \citep{letzelter2024wta,delattre2025cdq,cortes2025timemcl}, their training follows a rate--distortion curve \citep{perera2024amcl}, and their hard assignment distorts mode probabilities \citep{wu2026wtagmm}. \citet{bennett2026freedom} derives the optimal mode probabilities of min-of-$K$, which we confirm. \citet{makabe2026esp} and Branch-JEPA \citep{song2026branchjepa} prefer energy-score training to hard assignment, but in the regime of Section~\ref{sec:es} such a generator memorizes.

\textbf{World models in feature space.} DINO-WM, DINO-world and V-JEPA~2 predict frozen features deterministically \citep{zhou2025dinowm,baldassarre2025dinoworld,assran2025vjepa2}. FlowWM \citep{porcher2026flowwm} applies flow matching to DINOv3 features and introduces Bouncing Shapes, which we adopt, and VFMF \citep{boduljak2025vfmf} and \citet{walker2025generalist} use diffusion on compressed or frozen features. Recent one-step stochastic world models \citep{lu2026driftworld,agibot2026geact,lee2026beckmann,park2026priming,hsu2026vsf} do not, to our knowledge, measure mode coverage on enumerable futures, and \citet{lee2026beckmann} report no planning gain on Push-T. Generated latents leave the manifold of real-image features \citep{zheng2025rae,boduljak2025vfmf,karypidis2026latentforesight}, and we measure this per objective.

\textbf{Memorization and rollout training.} Conditional diffusion models memorize more when the condition is more informative \citep{gu2025memorization,kim2026memorization}. Data copying shows as a local overrepresentation of training points \citep{meehan2020nonparametric,somepalli2023diffusion,carlini2023extracting}. Conditional GANs that ignore their noise input show a related mode collapse \citep{yang2019dsgan,mao2019msgan}, and with unlimited capacity a model trained with any sample-based objective memorizes \citep{li2018imle}. Our measurement labels outcomes by mode and compares training with held-out contexts. Training on self-generated contexts is the classical remedy for compounding error \citep{ross2011dagger,venkatraman2015dad,bengio2015scheduled,lamb2016professor,hafner2019planet,chen2024diffusionforcing,huang2025selfforcing,gao2026sword}. Closest-among-top-$K$ fine-tuning \citep{zhang2025catk} rolls a tokenized policy out on the candidate nearest the true trajectory, and our per-step matching is its continuous-feature analog. \citet{terver2025drives} and \citet{cheung2026fidelity} find that multi-step rollout losses and single-step prediction fidelity are poor predictors of planning success, and our planning results agree.
